About Riverside Academy

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Our History

Founded in 1985, Riverside Academy has been a beacon of educational excellence for over 35 years. What started as a small private school with just 50 students has grown into a thriving institution serving over 850 students from kindergarten through 12th grade.

Our campus, located in the heart of Riverside, California, spans 25 acres of beautifully landscaped grounds featuring state-of-the-art facilities including modern classrooms, science laboratories, art studios, a performing arts center, and comprehensive athletic facilities.

Our Mission

To provide an exceptional educational experience that challenges students academically, nurtures their creativity, develops their character, and prepares them to be leaders and innovators in an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To be the leading independent school in Southern California, recognized for academic excellence, innovative teaching, and graduating students who make meaningful contributions to society.

Our Values

  • Academic Excellence
  • Integrity and Character
  • Innovation and Creativity
  • Community and Collaboration
  • Global Citizenship
