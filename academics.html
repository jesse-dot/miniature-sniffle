Academic Programs

Rigorous Curriculum, Exceptional Results

Our Curriculum

Riverside Academy offers a comprehensive college-preparatory curriculum designed to challenge and inspire students at every level. Our programs emphasize critical thinking, creativity, and real-world application of knowledge.

Academic Divisions

Lower School (K-5)

Our Lower School program builds a strong foundation in core subjects while nurturing curiosity and a love of learning. Students benefit from:

  • Small class sizes (12-15 students)
  • Integrated curriculum combining subjects
  • Daily art, music, and physical education
  • Focus on social-emotional development
  • Technology integration from kindergarten

Middle School (6-8)

The Middle School program provides a bridge between elementary and high school, with:

  • Departmentalized instruction by subject specialists
  • Advanced placement options in math and language
  • Project-based learning opportunities
  • Leadership development programs
  • Comprehensive advisory system
  • Community service requirements

Upper School (9-12)

Our college-preparatory Upper School offers:

  • 25+ Advanced Placement (AP) courses
  • Honors tracks in all core subjects
  • Independent study opportunities
  • College counseling from freshman year
  • Internship and research programs
  • Dual enrollment with local universities

Academic Departments

Mathematics

From Algebra to Calculus BC, our math program develops problem-solving skills and logical thinking. We offer advanced courses including Multivariable Calculus and Linear Algebra.

Science

State-of-the-art labs support courses in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Environmental Science. Students engage in hands-on experiments and research projects.

English & Literature

Our English curriculum develops strong writing, critical reading, and analytical skills through classic and contemporary literature.

History & Social Studies

Courses include World History, U.S. History, Government, Economics, and specialized topics like Constitutional Law and International Relations.

World Languages

Students can study Spanish, French, Mandarin Chinese, or Latin, with opportunities for study abroad and cultural exchange programs.

Computer Science

Comprehensive CS curriculum including programming, web development, artificial intelligence, and robotics competitions.

Special Programs & Opportunities

STEM Excellence Program

Advanced track for students passionate about science, technology, engineering, and mathematics with research mentorships and competition teams.

Arts Conservatory

Intensive programs in visual arts, music, theater, and dance with opportunities for portfolio development and performances.

Global Studies Certificate

Interdisciplinary program combining language study, international relations, and cultural immersion experiences.

Entrepreneurship Program

Students develop business plans, participate in pitch competitions, and launch real ventures with mentorship from local business leaders.
