📍 Campus Location

1250 Education Drive, Riverside, CA 92501

Get Directions

Directions

From Los Angeles (I-10 East)

  • Take I-10 East to I-60 East
  • Exit at Market Street
  • Turn left onto Market Street
  • Continue for 2 miles
  • Turn right onto Education Drive

From San Diego (I-15 North)

  • Take I-15 North to CA-91 West
  • Exit at Market Street
  • Turn right onto Market Street
  • Continue for 1.5 miles
  • Turn right onto Education Drive

Parking: Visitor parking is available in Lot A near the main entrance. Please check in at the security office upon arrival.
