Join Our Community of Learners

Welcome to Riverside Academy Admissions

We are delighted that you are considering Riverside Academy for your child's education. Our admissions process is designed to help us get to know your family and for you to learn more about our school community.

We seek students who are intellectually curious, motivated to learn, and ready to contribute positively to our school community. We welcome applications from families who share our values and are committed to academic excellence.

Application Process

1

Attend an Information Session

Join us for a virtual or in-person information session to learn about our programs, philosophy, and campus. Sessions are held monthly from September through January.

2

Submit Online Application

Complete our online application form, including:

  • Student information and academic history
  • Parent/guardian information
  • Student essay (grades 6-12)
  • Application fee: $150
3

Submit Supporting Materials

Required documents include:

  • Current and previous year transcripts
  • Two teacher recommendations
  • Standardized test scores (ISEE or SSAT for grades 6-12)
4

Student Visit & Assessment

Applicants will be invited to campus for:

  • Campus tour with current students
  • Classroom visit (K-5)
  • Student interview (grades 6-12)
  • Academic assessment appropriate to grade level
5

Parent Interview

All applicant families meet with members of our admissions team to discuss your child's educational goals and learn more about our school community.

6

Admissions Decision

Decisions are sent via email and postal mail. Admitted families will receive enrollment contracts and information about next steps.

Tuition & Financial Aid

2025-2026 Tuition

Lower School (K-5) $28,500 per year
Middle School (6-8) $32,000 per year
Upper School (9-12) $35,500 per year

Tuition includes most academic materials, technology fees, and standard field trips. Additional fees may apply for certain activities and programs.

Financial Aid

Riverside Academy is committed to making our program accessible to qualified students from diverse economic backgrounds. We offer need-based financial aid to approximately 25% of our students.

  • Awards range from partial to full tuition assistance
  • Financial aid decisions are separate from admissions decisions
  • Applications are confidential and processed through School and Student Services (SSS)
  • Financial aid application deadline: February 1, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average class size?

Our average class size is 15 students, with a maximum of 18 in Lower School and 20 in Middle and Upper School.

Do you offer transportation?

We offer bus service from select neighborhoods within a 15-mile radius. Transportation fees apply.

What is your acceptance rate?

We typically accept 60-70% of applicants. However, this varies by grade level based on available spaces.

Can students transfer mid-year?

We accept mid-year transfers on a space-available basis. Please contact our admissions office to inquire about current availability.
